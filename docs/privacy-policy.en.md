# Privacy Policy

**App:** Habbits Line
**Effective date:** 6 September 2026

## In short

Habbits Line collects no data. The app runs entirely on your device: it has no accounts,
no servers, and makes no network requests. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

## What the app stores

Everything you create yourself is kept locally, in a database on the device:

- habits — name, emoji, color, schedule, and reminder time;
- daily completion entries;
- expenses — amount, date, category, and note;
- expense categories and the budget for a period;
- app settings — theme, language, and the day a period starts.

This data never leaves the device. The app does not send it anywhere and has no
technical means to: it contains no network calls at all.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, no sign-up.
- No analytics, usage statistics, or crash reporting.
- No advertising.
- No tracking, and no third-party advertising or analytics libraries.
- No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.
- No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

## Notifications

Reminders are iOS local notifications. They are scheduled on the device itself, and the
reminder text (the habit's name) never leaves it. The app has no push notifications, so
it has no push token either. Notification permission is requested only when you turn on
your first reminder, and you can revoke it in iOS Settings — the app keeps working
without reminders.

## Backups

Settings let you export a backup: a JSON file holding all of your data. The file is
created on the device, then iOS shows its standard share sheet and **you** decide what
happens next — save it to Files, send it to yourself, or put it in a cloud service. This
is the only way data can leave the device, and it always starts with an explicit action
of yours.

Once you hand the file to a service, it is governed by that service's rules; the app's
developer gets no access to it.

Import is the mirror image: you pick a file through the system dialog and the app reads
it locally.

## Device backup

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, the app's database is included in
that backup along with the rest of the device's data. That process is run by Apple, not
by the app, and is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its database with it. Individual habits, expenses, and
categories are deleted inside the app. Nothing remains on the developer's side, because
nothing ever arrived there.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever behaves differently, this page will be updated along with the app, and
the effective date will change.
